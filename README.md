# Neck CT anatomy trainer

Educational tool for learning head and neck anatomy on CT. **Not a medical device and not for clinical use.**

Scan: one case from Blagosklonova E, et al. *Open dataset of annotated DICOM images of head CTA for intracranial
aneurysm detection.* Zenodo, 2025. doi:10.5281/zenodo.15697196, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Data paper: doi:10.3390/data11040074. The case was cropped, resampled to 0.6 mm and anatomy outlines were added;
the original authors do not endorse this tool.

Outlines drafted with TotalSegmentator (Apache-2.0; Wasserthal et al., Radiology: Artificial Intelligence 2023), built on
nnU-Net (Isensee et al., Nature Methods 2021). 3D view: three.js (MIT). Full credits are in the app under "About and credits".
